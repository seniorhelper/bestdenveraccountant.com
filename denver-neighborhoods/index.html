Why your address matters

A 'Denver' mailing address doesn't always mean Denver city limits. Sales tax, the occupational privilege tax and business licensing all depend on the actual jurisdiction. That's especially true around the edges of the city and in the Tech Center.

Neighborhood guide

RiNo (River North Art District)

Murals, breweries, design studios and creative agencies. Lots of young businesses with fast growth and messy early books.

LoDo

Union Station, offices and restaurants. Professional services firms and hospitality groups with multi-location payroll.

Cherry Creek

Retail, medical practices and wealth management. High-income owners where tax planning pays off most.

Highlands

Restaurants, boutiques and a lot of short-term rentals. Lodger's tax and STR licensing come up constantly.

Capitol Hill

Nonprofits, bars and dense apartment living. Nonprofit filings and hospitality payroll are common needs.

Five Points

Historic Black business district with a growing restaurant and arts scene. Small, independent owners who need flexible help.

Washington Park

Homeowners, landlords and independent professionals. Rental property and home office questions lead the list.

Baker / South Broadway

Vintage shops, music venues and makers. Sales tax collection and inventory tracking matter here.

Central Park

Families, new construction and home-based businesses. First-time business owners often start here.

Denver Tech Center

Corporate offices that straddle Denver, Greenwood Village and Centennial. Which city you're actually in changes which occupational privilege tax applies.

Outside Denver?

For Aurora, Boulder, Colorado Springs and the rest of the state, see Colorado CPA help by city.

Common questions

How do I know if my business is inside Denver city limits?

Use the City and County of Denver's address lookup or check your property record. Mailing addresses that say Denver aren't always inside the city.

Do neighborhoods have different tax rates?

Neighborhoods inside Denver share the city's rates, but special districts and nearby cities can change the combined rate, so always look up rates by exact address.
