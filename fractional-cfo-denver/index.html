The questions a CFO answers

Can we afford this hire?

A 12-month cash forecast shows what the payroll does to your runway before you make the offer.

Why is cash tight if sales are up?

Receivables, inventory and growth itself eat cash. The cash flow statement shows exactly where it went.

Are we priced right?

Margin by product, client or job shows who's paying the bills and who's quietly costing you.

Are we ready for a loan or investors?

Clean statements, a forecast and a story the numbers back up.

Common questions

When does a business need a fractional CFO?

Usually when growth decisions start getting expensive: hiring, raising money, adding a location, or when cash feels tight even though sales are up.

What's the difference between a bookkeeper and a fractional CFO?

The bookkeeper records what happened. The CFO uses those records to forecast what will happen and recommend what to do about it.

How is a fractional CFO paid?

Typically a monthly retainer for a set scope, which costs a fraction of a full-time executive's salary and benefits.
