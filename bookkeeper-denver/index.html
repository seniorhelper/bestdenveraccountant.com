Denver businesses have extra lines to track

A Denver bookkeeping file needs a few accounts a generic setup won't have: Denver sales tax payable, the Denver occupational privilege tax (both the employee and business halves), FAMLI premiums, and possibly lodger's or food and beverage taxes for hospitality businesses. Common questions

How much bookkeeping does a small Denver business need?

Most need a monthly close: transactions categorized, accounts reconciled and reports reviewed. High-volume retail or restaurants may need weekly attention.

Can you fix books someone else messed up?

Yes. A cleanup review finds the problems, then accounts are corrected and reconciled so your tax return is built on real numbers.

Why track Denver sales tax separately?

Denver collects its own sales tax apart from the state. Keeping the liabilities separate makes each return easy and shows exactly what's owed to whom.
