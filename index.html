Denver accounting questions

What does a Denver accountant do for a small business?

Keeps the books accurate, runs or reviews payroll, files business and owner tax returns, and handles Denver-specific items like the occupational privilege tax and city sales tax.

Is Denver's sales tax different from the rest of Colorado?

Yes. Denver is a home-rule city that collects its own sales and use tax at a 5.15% city rate, on top of state and district taxes, so Denver businesses file with the city directly.

How quickly can I get help?

Same day in most cases. Penny answers right now, and the team calls back fast. Urgent IRS or payroll problems go to the front of the line.

Do you only work with businesses?

Mostly businesses and their owners, but individual returns for Denver residents, landlords and short-term rental hosts are welcome too.
